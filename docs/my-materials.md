## Against the Blacksmithing plan, 33 to 150

| Material | Plan needs | You have | Short or spare |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Copper (ore, or bars at one each) | 496 | 364 ore + 73 bars = 437 | **59 short** |
| Tin Ore | 170 | 255 | 85 spare |
| Rough Stone | 92 | 286 | 194 spare |
| Coarse Stone | 76 | 210 | 134 spare |
| Silver Bar | 6 | 6 bars, plus 23 ore | covered, 23 ore spare |
| Iron Bar | 25 | 1 Iron Ore | **24 short** |
| Heavy Stone | 6 | 1 | **5 short** |
| Strong Flux, Medium Leather | 11 each | none counted | **buy** |
| Green Dye | 3 | none | **buy** |

So for 33 to 150 you mostly need about 59 more Copper Ore (or Copper Bars), 24 Iron Ore, 5 Heavy Stone, 11 Strong Flux, 11 Medium Leather and 3 Green Dye. Your spare Tin, Rough Stone and Coarse Stone can be sold, or kept for the next stretch. The app's Leveling plan prices all of it at your house and tells you whether selling the spare stone beats keeping it.
