# Blacksmithing leveling plan, WoW Forever

Built from the YouTube guide Chip shared (November 19), checked against the live game tables for build 1.60.1.70959: every recipe, its materials and its skill colors come from the game data on wago.tools, and learn ranks and trainer costs from players' recorded trainer windows (EG Link). Where the data disagrees with the guide, the data wins and the change is called out. Patches land daily, so if a color in game differs, trust the game.

**Chip right now:** Blacksmithing 33, Mining 145. Near term goal: 150. Your own ore, stone, copper and tin count as free. The app's Crafting section has a live version of this plan with a shopping list that subtracts what you own and prices the rest at your house.

## How to read the numbers

- **Colors:** below **yellow** the recipe is orange and every craft gives a point. Between yellow and grey the chance falls in a straight line from certain to zero (the Classic rule, approximate). **Green** is the midpoint. **Grey** gives nothing.
- **Crafts** is the expected number of crafts for the step, adding up 1 divided by the chance at each skill point. Real luck varies a few crafts either way. The Working Overtime legacy talent raises the odds and lowers these counts.
- **Trainer ranks:** Journeyman at level 10 caps you at 125, Expert at level 20 caps at 200, Artisan at level 35 from Brikk Keencraft in Booty Bay caps at 300.

## Where the data changes the guide

1. **Silver Skeleton Key** takes 1 Silver Bar *and* 1 Rough Grinding Stone per key. That is why the guide says to keep some Rough Grinding Stones.
2. **Patterned Bronze Bracers (140 to 147)** are already yellow at 125 and half way to grey at 140, so the 7 points cost about 19 crafts. **Bronze Warhammer is learnable at 100 and stays orange until 155**, so extending the warhammers from 129 to 147 is a point every craft (about 8 more crafts, each 8 Bronze Bar, 1 Strong Flux, 1 Medium Leather). Check Strong Flux and Medium Leather prices: if they are cheap, warhammers win. The app compares both at your house's prices.
3. **Rough Bronze Leggings at 80** is right for Forever: trainers teach it at 80 (Classic was later).
4. **Iron Buckle** goes grey at 130, so 125 to 129 is about 7 crafts, matching the guide's six.
5. **"Mithril Scale Bracers" does not exist** in this build. The guide's 200 to 205 step is Heavy Mithril Gauntlet, the same recipe it uses 210 to 215.
6. **Green Iron Leggings costs 50s to learn** at the trainer. Budget for it at 155.
7. **Mithril for 183 to 257** adds up to about 1220 Mithril Bars by this route, which agrees with the guide's warning that the 200s are the expensive stretch.

## Selling what you craft

The guide is right that Green Iron, Golden Scale and the Mithril pieces are gear people wear. The Crafting section shows each one's price, how many sell a day and the best listing length, so you can sell the leveling output instead of vendoring it. Sharpening and grinding stones sell too.

## Your materials

Enter what you have in the app (Crafting, My materials). The plan treats them as free, but flags any you would make more money selling, then buying the bars or stones you need instead.
